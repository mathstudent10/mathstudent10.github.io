Class 10 Math

Chapter 1: Real Numbers

Question 1

Euclid Division Algorithm ka istemal kar ke HCF nikaalain.

Solution:

Step 1: Bari number ko choti number se divide karein۔
Step 2: Remainder ko dobara divisor se divide karein۔
Step 3: Jab remainder zero ho jaye to divisor HCF hota hai۔

Answer: HCF = 7

Question 2

Do numbers ka HCF aur LCM maloom karein.

Solution:

Step 1: Dono numbers ke prime factors likhein۔
Step 2: Common factors se HCF nikaalein۔
Step 3: Tamam factors se LCM nikaalein۔

Answer: LCM = 84
